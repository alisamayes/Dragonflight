# Red Dragon - "Redgon"
## Dragon Stats
Health - 500
Attack - 120
Defence - 90
Flight Range - 15
Speed - 8
## Draconic Abilities
Lvl 5 - Passive - No CD - Constant - “Flame buffer”: Redgon's intense heat and firepower accumulates as he fighters, allow him to incinerate large forces as the heat burns up everything around it. For every round of combat, Redgon gains a stacking 3% damage buff that multiplies the damage her deals, up to a total of 30%
Lvl 10 - Ability - 1 turn CD- Instant - “Plasma Lance”: Redgon fires a highly concentrated beam of plasma at a tile, instantly scorching anything on it for 100% of his attack. It ignores enemies defence.
Lvl 15 - Ability - 3 turn CD -  3 hours - “Fiery Malice”: Redgon focuses their resolve on accomplishing their will and sets themselves ablaze. Fire courses through their veins and for the next 3 hours their attack, flight range and speed increase by 50%. They gain an extra charge of Plasma Lance for that day.

# Black Dragon - “Blackgon”
## Dragon Stats
Health - 500
Attack - 100
Defence - 140
Flight Range - 10
Speed - 4
## Draconic Abilities
Lvl 5 - Passive - No CD - Constant - “Spiked Scales”: Blackgons ancient age has meant their scales have hardened over the centuries into a blade like, sharp edged scales and in some places spikes. Enemies struggle to pierce it and each attack costs them. Gain Thorns damage. Each time an enemy strikes Blackgon they also take 10% of Blackgon’s defence as damage.
Lvl 10 - Ability - 1 turn CD - 12 hours - “Ancient’s Roar”: Blackgon lets out a blood-chilling roar that instils fear into the hearts of all those around him (his flight range). All enemies within range get debuffed for causing them to dealing 30% less damage and if they’re an army their movement speed is also reduced by 40% for their next movement.
Lvl 15 - Ability - 3 turn CD - 3 hours - “Defend the Citadel”: The ancient black Dragon returns to the Citadel with fervour, using 1/3rd of the normal time cost and ignores range restrictions. He then increases his defence stat by 50%

# Green Dragon - “Greengon”
## Dragon Stats
Health - 600
Attack - 80
Defence - 100
Flight Range - 10
Speed - 6
## Draconic Abilities
Lvl 5 - Passive - No CD - Constant - “Healing Crystal”: Greengon’s inept prowess at healing magic is able to constantly heal their wounds. For every 1 hour expended, heal 2% health.
Lvl 10 - Ability - 1 turn CD - 5 hours - “Draconic Resurgence”: Only usable if under 50% max health. Greengon floods heir body with a surge of healing magic and invigorates themselves. Instantly restore 25% hp and double the passive healing for the next hours. 
Lvl 15 - Ability 3 turn CD - 5 Hours - “Vevify”: For the rest of the turn immediately gain + 20% map hp. While vivify is active is active, each time greengon attacks they can choose to sacrifice 20% of their current HP to add that much onto their next attack as attack power. E.g. sacrificing 100hp = +100 attack on next damage round.

# Yellow Dragon - “Yellowgon”
## Dragon Stats
Health - 500
Attack - 90
Defence - 90
Flight Range - 12
Speed - 5
## Draconic Abilities
Lvl 5 - Passive -  No CD - Constant - “Foresight”: Yellowgon’s ability to see the flow of time allows them to see telegraphed attacks before they happen and mitigate them. After each combat round, undo 10% of all damage taken.
Lvl 10 - Ability -  1 turn CD - 1 hour - “Timestop”: Yellowgon freezes the sands of time in place for a short moment. For the next hour no time is consumed and enemies are frozen and unable to retaliate.
Lvl 15 - Ability - 5 turn CD - 24 hours - “Chrono-conic pulse”: A wave of advanced chronomancy ripples throughout the map. Everything slows down. For this turn, armies have their move speed halved, settlements have their growth speed halved and damaged settlements only loose half of their eco power

# Purple Dragon - “Purplegon”
## Dragon Stats
Health - 500
Attack - 120
Defence - 120
Flight Range - 8
Speed - 4
## Draconic Abilities
Lvl 5 - Passive - No CD - Constant - “Ice Talons”: Purplegon mastery of elemental magic allows them  to imbue their claws with ice. Enemies struck by Purplegon have their attacks slowed, causing them to loose 10% of their attack from each hit. 
Lvl 10 - Ability - 1 turn CD - Instant - “Tempest Strike”: Purplegon harnesses their storm magic to strike a tile, instantly dealing 100% of their attack to the enemy. If there is another enemy it will bounce to them with a 50% damage reduction in effect, until now new enemies are nearby.
Lvl 15 - Ability - 3 turn CD - Instant - “Absolute Zero Breath”: Purplegon unleashes a 5 tile long attack at targeted direction of bone chilling frost. Enemies struck by it take 150% of purplegons attack as damage and are no longer able to recover HP.

# Brown Dragon - “Browngon”
## Dragon Stats
Health - 550
Attack - 100
Defence - 110
Flight Range - 8
Speed - 2
## Draconic Abilities
Lvl 5 - Passive - No CD - Constant - Mountain’s Boon: Browngon gains strength from the nearby peaks. If fighting with a mountain within 3 tiles of range, gain a +2 to speed and +10% to attack.
Lvl 10 - Ability - 1 turn CD - 24 hours  - “Tremors”: Browngon causes a localized earthquake around the target location within range, shaking the earth on one tile that gains the “lose ground” status. Any fight occurring on the tile will inflict a debuff on hostile armies the reduces their defence by 15%. 
Lvl 15 - Ability - 3 turn CD - Constant - “Terrascape”: Browngon uses their mastery over the earth to raise a mountain from the ground, permanently altering the landscape. (Cannot fully close off a route for settlements to the citadel and must have at least 1 space between any new mountain tile and settlements/ the citadel.
